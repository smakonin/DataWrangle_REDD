{\fontsize{18}{20}\selectfont\bfseries REDD-Only Evidence and NILM Deployment Claims\par}
{\fontsize{10}{12}\selectfont Stephen Makonin\quad Technical note version 1.2\quad 25 October 2026\par}
\vspace{3pt}
REDD helped establish open, reproducible NILM research and remains valuable for benchmark comparisons. \textbf{High REDD scores alone do not establish broad deployment performance.} This note addresses evidence needed across households, seasons, and measurement systems.

\textbf{Fresh audit of the original low-frequency release}

All 122 canonical files (116 channels and six label files) matched the locally archived Version 1.0 copy byte for byte. The audit parsed \textbf{56,341,629 records from six homes}. Independent checks verified that the current importer preserved all \textbf{14,113,792 original mains records}, including their source line numbers.

The timestamp union contains 7,755,931 rows. The 25.47\% ratio measures exact timestamp coincidence; native cadence contributes to it. It is neither uptime nor sensor loss. Residuals remain in source units and do not establish meter error. No missing group constituent occurred among the historical importer's row candidates.

\textbf{Temporal coverage depends on the protocol.} Modal observed intervals are 1~s for mains in houses 1--5 and 2~s in house 6; circuits have modes of 4~s in houses 1/5 and 3~s elsewhere. Connecting consecutive observations only when at most 60~s apart, then intersecting those spans across all channels, gives shared-span fractions of each first-to-last mains envelope of \textbf{50.30, 39.70, 37.94, 39.41, 8.23, and 55.00\%} (houses 1--6). This threshold-defined duration proxy is not measured continuous uptime.

\textbf{Data integrity checks pass; gaps, cadence, and selection still constrain evaluation.} The archived 2015 log's 2,858 mains repairs belong to \textbf{Stephen Makonin's importer, not REDD's acquisition pipeline}. The current importer performs no such repairs. The audit establishes no particular NILM accuracy effect.

\textbf{What credible validation should include}

Report channels, units, active/apparent power, quality masks, preprocessing, shared exposure, and activations. Keep test targets out of aggregate repair. Test independently measured mains, held-out homes, and another acquisition system; report appliance energy/event errors and prediction delay. Mixed circuits are not individual-appliance ground truth.

\textbf{Complementary data by purpose.} Use \textbf{AMPds2} and \textbf{R1Hz} for longitudinal energy tests, selected calibrated \textbf{UK-DALE} mains for measurement checks, and \textbf{REFIT} for household diversity. The choices imply no overall accuracy ranking. Apply the same missingness and quality scrutiny to every dataset; retain R1Hz s/+ flags and score imputed references separately.

\textbf{Disclosure.} Stephen Makonin coauthored the AMPds2, RAE, and R1Hz dataset publications.
