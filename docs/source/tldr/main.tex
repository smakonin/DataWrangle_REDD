{\fontsize{18}{20}\selectfont\bfseries REDD-Only Evidence and NILM Deployment Claims\par}
{\fontsize{10}{12}\selectfont Stephen Makonin\quad Technical note version 1.1\quad 25 October 2026\par}
\vspace{3pt}
REDD helped establish open, reproducible NILM research and remains valuable for benchmark comparisons. \textbf{High REDD scores alone do not establish broad deployment performance.} This note addresses the additional evidence needed for claims across households, seasons, and measurement systems.

\textbf{Limits to address in deployment claims}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Coverage.} Six homes with short, uneven valid records provide limited evidence across households and seasons. Many correlated samples do not increase household diversity.
\item \textbf{Timing.} Mains readings are nominally one second apart; circuit readings approximately three seconds. Verify alignment and recording overlap. Upsampling adds no measured information.
\item \textbf{Missing readings.} Filling, smoothing, excluding, and joining observations can change transitions, labels, and test difficulty, raising or lowering scores.
\item \textbf{Reference compatibility.} Check active versus apparent power, units, and channel aggregation. A circuit containing several devices is not individual-appliance ground truth.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{A case study of this repository's historical importer}

\textbf{The repairs belong to Stephen Makonin's archived 2015 importer, not REDD's acquisition pipeline.} The current Python 3 importer does not perform those repairs. The following counts describe the archived log.

No raw-data rerun was performed. Native circuit cadence contributes to selection: 25.47\% is neither uptime nor sensor loss. Repair labels are not measured clock errors. Fallback magnitudes cover only those 476 cases. These counts establish no particular accuracy effect.

\textbf{What credible validation should include}

Report channels, units, timestamps, quality masks, preprocessing, valid shared exposure, and activations. Keep test targets out of aggregate repair. Test independently measured mains, held-out homes, and another acquisition system; report appliance energy/event errors and when predictions become available.

\textbf{Complementary data by purpose.} Use \textbf{AMPds2} and \textbf{R1Hz} for longitudinal energy tests, selected calibrated \textbf{UK-DALE} mains for measurement checks, and \textbf{REFIT} for household diversity. The choices imply no overall accuracy ranking. Apply the same missingness and quality scrutiny to every dataset; retain R1Hz s/+ flags and score imputed references separately.

\textbf{Disclosure.} Stephen Makonin coauthored the AMPds2, RAE, and R1Hz dataset publications.
